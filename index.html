Climate Memory + Evidence

Start with something you or another longtime resident has noticed about the local climate. Then use climate data to see how well that observation lines up with the long-term record.

You will:

remember or interview → choose one observation → find matching data → compare experience with evidence → save the result to your Garden Journal.

The question

Does the climate record match what we remember?

People notice real changes in the places where they live and garden. But memory usually covers a shorter period, a smaller area, or a particular season. Climate records let us check those observations against measurements collected over many years.

This is not a right-or-wrong test. The data may support an observation, complicate it, or leave the question open.

You will begin in one of two ways: reflect on your own experience, or interview someone who has lived in the area long enough to notice changes.
