Evidence + experience

How does the climate record compare?

Your goal is not to decide whether someone's memory was “right” or “wrong.” Decide how well the available evidence lines up with the observation.

Why might experience and climate data differ?
  • A person's observation may cover a shorter period than the climate record.
  • Conditions can vary substantially from year to year.
  • A garden's microclimate may differ from the nearest weather station.
  • Precipitation totals do not necessarily show when the rain or snow occurred.
  • Some climate changes are easier to detect confidently than others.
