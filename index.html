💭 Memory + 📈 evidence

Does the climate record match what we remember?

Our memories are useful clues, especially when we’ve watched the same place for years. Climate records give us another view: measurements collected over decades. The record might echo the memory, tell a more complicated story, or leave us with a new question. All three are useful findings.

●No “gotcha” answers here. This exercise is about considering climate evidence alongside an observation—not finding conclusive proof that the observation is right or wrong. The data may support what you noticed, complicate the story, or raise new questions.

First, decide whose memories you want to start with — your own, or someone else’s.
