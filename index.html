💭 Memory + 📈 evidence

Does the climate record match what we remember?

Our memories are useful clues, especially when we’ve watched the same place for years. Climate records give us another view: measurements collected over decades. Put them together and we can ask a surprisingly interesting question.

●No “gotcha” answers here. The record might echo the memory, tell a more complicated story, or leave us with a new question. All three are useful findings.

First, decide whose memories you want to start with — your own, or someone else’s.
